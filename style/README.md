# style

How you write. `/career-setup` creates:

- `exemplars/`: your own past writing, one file per document, with front
  matter saying what it is and when you wrote it. Drafts are always written
  with two of these as voice samples.
- `exemplar-index.md`: a table of the exemplars.
- `style-profile.md`: your voice in plain rules, plus the genre exemptions
  the voice pass must respect.
- `quarantine/`: documents from your inbox that weren't your own writing,
  such as pasted chatbot output, blank templates or someone else's CV, each
  with the reason. These are never used as voice samples.

You own these files.
