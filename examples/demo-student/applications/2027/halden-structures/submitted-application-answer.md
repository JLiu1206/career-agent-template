Why do you want to join Halden Structures' graduate scheme? (200 words)

I spent last summer in Halden's bridges team, and the part I enjoyed most was the bearing checks. Not the checks themselves, which were slow, but working out how to make them faster. By the end of the internship my Python script ran the load-combination checks for 40 bearings in about three hours instead of two days, and the team has kept using it.

I want to come back as a graduate because I saw how much responsibility your engineers get early. The graduate on my team was leading inspections in her second year. I'd like that kind of steep start, with the chartership support your scheme offers alongside it.

I also want to stay in bridges. My final-year project on fatigue in 3D-printed titanium brackets has made me more interested in how connections fail over time, and bearings and joints are where a lot of real bridge maintenance happens.

I know the team, the tools and the pace. I'd like to spend the next few years getting properly good at the work rather than starting again somewhere new.
