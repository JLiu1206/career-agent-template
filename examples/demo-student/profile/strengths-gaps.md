# Strengths and gaps (fictional demo student)

Claude's assessment, written at setup, for Ellis to correct. It is blunt on
purpose.

## Strong for bridge and structural graduate schemes

- **A real bridges internship with a measurable result.** The Halden bearing
  script (two days to about three hours, 40 bearings) is specific, technical
  and still in use. It is the strongest story Ellis has.
- **Programming well above the usual for a mechanical engineer.** Python,
  MATLAB and a pipeline that a research group reused.
- **Academic record:** First in Year 2, Dean's List.
- **Coherent direction.** Footbridge project, Halden bridges team and a
  fatigue final-year project all point the same way.

## Thin

- **Leadership.** It rests on leading the analysis within a team of 6 and on a
  treasurer role. There is no story about leading people through
  disagreement. The wind-turbine story is the closest.
- **Client-facing or commercial work:** none recorded.
- **Site experience:** two inspections. Graduate schemes that stress site
  work will notice.
- **Wear.** `halden-bearing-script` is already used twice. Rotate in the
  footbridge or UROP stories where the question allows.

## What would close the gaps

- A story where Ellis persuaded or led others, written down while it's fresh.
- Any site or client exposure in the final year, such as an ICE student event
  or a site visit with the department.
