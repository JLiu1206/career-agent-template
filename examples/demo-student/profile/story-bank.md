## 1. `footbridge-structural-analysis`

Year 3 design project: led the structural analysis; the final design used 18% less steel.

- **Situation:** Six-week group design project, team of 6, footbridge for a river crossing that floods most winters. [src: inbox/cv.docx; inbox/Halden internship cover letter.docx]
- **Action:** Took on the structural analysis. The first design was safe but heavy, so Ellis ran the deck through ANSYS at three span arrangements; the team moved to a shallower truss. [src: inbox/Halden internship cover letter.docx]
- **Result:** 18% less steel than the baseline, still within the deflection limit. [src: inbox/cv.docx; inbox/Halden internship cover letter.docx]
- **Shows:** technical depth, analysis, teamwork, design judgement

## 3. `wind-turbine-pitch-fix`

Year 2 design module: tested a small pitch change before a full reprint.

- **Situation:** Team of four, small wind turbine with a target power output in the wind tunnel. Two days before testing, the blades stalled at low wind speeds, giving about half the target. [src: inbox/Corrin Rail teamwork answer.docx]
- **Action:** The team wanted to remake the blades. Ellis suggested checking a cheaper fix first, modelled the pitch angle in a spreadsheet, found that a 6 degree change should move the stall point below the test speed, and talked the team through the numbers. [src: inbox/Corrin Rail teamwork answer.docx]
- **Result:** 94% of the target output the next morning; the spare day went on the report. [src: inbox/Corrin Rail teamwork answer.docx]
- **Shows:** problem solving, teamwork, persuasion with evidence
- **Wear:** 1
