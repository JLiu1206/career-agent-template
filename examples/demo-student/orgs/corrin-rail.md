## What they do
Rail infrastructure consultancy with about 900 staff in Leeds, Birmingham and London. Its work covers:
- track renewals
- station upgrades
- assessing and strengthening Victorian rail bridges

## The scheme or role
- Graduate Scheme, Track and Structures. Two years, in four six-month placements:
  - track design
  - bridge assessment
  - site
  - one of the graduate's choice
- Supported route to IStructE or ICE chartership.
- Starts September 2027. Applications close 15 December 2026.

## Application process
1. Online form with two questions:
   - "Why do you want to join Corrin Rail's graduate scheme? (200 words)"
   - "Tell us about a time you improved how something was done. (150 words)"
2. Online tests: numerical and situational judgement.
3. Video interview.
4. Assessment centre: group exercise and technical interview.
