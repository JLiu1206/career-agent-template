Why I want this UROP

I am a first-year Mechanical Engineering student and I want to spend this summer finding out whether I enjoy research as much as I enjoy coursework.

The vibration project interests me because it connects to the part of my course I find hardest. Our dynamics lectures explain natural frequencies on paper, but I have never measured one. I would like to see how far a real composite panel sits from the textbook answer, and why.

I can offer steady programming. I taught myself Python in sixth form and this year I wrote a MATLAB script for our statics coursework that checked every member force in a truss rather than the three we were asked for. It found a sign error in my hand calculation that I would otherwise have submitted.

I am organised and reliable with time. I tutor two GCSE students every week alongside my degree and have not missed a session this year.

I would expect to spend the first weeks learning the rig and the analysis code, and I would be happy to take on the repetitive testing that a project like this needs. I would like to leave with a result the group can actually use.
