Dear Hiring Team,

I would like to apply for the 2026 Summer Internship in Halden Structures' bridges team.

Last term my design group had six weeks to design a footbridge for a river crossing that floods most winters. I took on the structural analysis. Our first design was safe but heavy, so I ran the deck through ANSYS at three span arrangements and we moved to a shallower truss. It used 18% less steel than our baseline and still met the deflection limit. That project is the reason I want to work on bridges rather than just study them.

Halden's work on the Avon cycle bridges is the kind of project I would like to learn from. A small span, a tight budget and a site that has to stay open. I also like that your interns join site inspections in their first month. I learn faster when I can see the thing I am calculating.

I can bring careful analysis and some programming. In my UROP last summer I wrote a MATLAB pipeline for modal analysis that the group still uses. I am comfortable with Python, ANSYS and SolidWorks, and I am used to explaining my working to people who have to check it.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be glad to talk about the footbridge project in more detail.

Kind regards,
Ellis Varga
